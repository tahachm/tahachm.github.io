I'm a Master of Mathematics (thesis) student in Computer Science at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/), advised by [Dr. Raouf Boutaba](https://rboutaba.cs.uwaterloo.ca). My work sits at the intersection of **systems, networks, and applied machine learning** — currently focused on terrestrial–non-terrestrial 5G integration and cross-layer optimization.

Before Waterloo, I earned a BSc in Computer Science with High Distinction from the [Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)](https://lums.edu.pk/), and spent a summer at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/) studying machine learning and technology entrepreneurship. Along the way I've worked in Singapore at [AICET](https://aicet.comp.nus.edu.sg/projects/), building [SoftMark](https://www.softmark.org), a digital grading platform deployed at NUS and across high schools in Singpaore, undertaken research at the intersection of LLMs and systems, notably benchmarking an [LLM response-caching architecture](/publications/), and led a new product's development as a software engineer at a fast-paced startup, [GreatFrontEnd](https://www.greatfrontend.com).

I'm broadly interested in how large language models reshape software engineering, networked systems, and the way we build things. I write about these ideas, and review papers I find interesting (or provocative), on my [blog](/blog/). Take a look at my [CV](/cv/) for the full picture, and feel free to [reach out](mailto:tahacheema95@gmail.com).
