As a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Waterloo, I work on integrating **terrestrial and non-terrestrial (NTN) 5G** networks within a cross-functional CS/ECE team.

I abstracted a testbed design that uses cross-layer information sharing to drive optimizations across **scheduling, beamforming, and transport**. The resulting testbed diagram became the reference artifact for explaining our approach in stakeholder meetings, and I led weekly project presentations for the CS team.

Drawing on my experience with agentic coding, I mapped the testbed proposals onto open-source 5G stacks — [Open5GS](https://open5gs.org/), [srsRAN](https://www.srslte.com/), and [OpenAirInterface](https://openairinterface.org/) — enabling the ECE team to implement non-terrestrial channel models with GNU Radio.
