[SoftMark](https://www.softmark.org) is a productionized digital grading platform deployed at the National University of Singapore (NUS) and secondary schools across Singapore. I built it as a Software Engineering Intern at the [AI Centre for Educational Technologies](https://aicet.comp.nus.edu.sg).

Two contributions I'm particularly proud of:

- A **trie-based algorithm** layered over open-source OCR to detect question numbering on scanned answer sheets, which doubled teacher productivity by automatically filtering out unattempted questions.
- An intuitive **2D grid allocation component** that replaced a linear menu, enabling fair distribution of grading workload among multiple graders.

**Skills:** React, TypeScript, Golang, PostgreSQL, and Material UI.
